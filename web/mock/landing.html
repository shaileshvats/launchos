Questions

Where do the beans come from?

We partner with a Bengaluru roaster who sources single-origin beans from Karnataka and Kerala estates.

Can we pause deliveries?

Yes. Pause for up to four weeks from the dashboard or with /brewloop pause.

Do you need any equipment from us?

No. Beans come ready to grind or pre-ground for your brewing method. We do not sell machines.
